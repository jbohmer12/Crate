Fill the frame with the center label, in good light, with the text sharp.

Not sure which pressing? Photograph your record’s label and Crate compares it with the Discogs photos of the top results. A white label is matched by details like stamps, handwriting and etching, so treat it as a hint. This uses 2 scans.

To pin down the exact pressing, photograph the etched text in the smooth ring near the center (the runout groove). Tilt the record so the light rakes across the letters.

Pick the exact pressing (check year, country and catalog number). Value and rarity are different for every pressing.
